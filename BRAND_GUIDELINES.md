# LX Obsidian Labs — Brand Guidelines

## Official identity
The approved visual identity is a silver/white geometric LX monogram with an electric-lime accent on an obsidian-black field. Preserve the approved original artwork: do not redraw, recolour, distort or substitute it without explicit approval.

## Colour palette
- Obsidian Black: `#080808` (primary background)
- Electric Lime: `#C6FF00` (accent)
- Off White: `#F4F4F4` (text)
- Silver: `#A8ADB4` (secondary)

## Typography
- Suggested display: Space Grotesk
- Suggested body: Inter

## Usage
Keep clear space around the logo. Use the approved original as the source of truth; derived transparent raster assets are convenience exports, not vector masters. Avoid stretching, gradients over the logo, or altered accent colours.

## Asset status
Brand kit raster assets exist in the working kit and have **not yet been committed** to this repository. Do not treat placeholder assets as approved originals.
